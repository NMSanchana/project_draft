# Pricing and Discount Guidelines

## Discount approval limits
Sales staff may give a discount of up to 10% without approval. Discounts between 10% and 20% need manager approval. Any discount above 20% requires sign-off from the director.

## Price floor
Never price an item below cost plus an 8% margin floor. Exceptions are only allowed for clearance stock approved in writing.

## Clearance
Items that remain unsold for 180 days may be marked down by up to 40%. Clearance prices are reviewed every month.

## Seasonal promotions
Black Friday promotions may discount electronics by up to 25%. Seasonal promotions must be announced at least 7 days in advance.

## Bundles and cross-selling
A bundle discount of 7% may be offered when two or more complementary products are sold together, for example a monitor with an HDMI cable.

## Price changes
List prices may change only once per quarter for each product. Competitor price matching is allowed within 5 days of purchase and requires proof of the competing price.
