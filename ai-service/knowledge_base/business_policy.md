# Business Policy

## Returns and refunds
Customers may return most items within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt. Electronics have a shorter 15-day return window, and opened software is not refundable. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

## Shipping
Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and costs $15 per order.

## Warranty
Electronics carry a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Appliances carry a 24-month warranty. Damage caused by misuse is not covered.

## Loyalty programme
Customers reach Silver status after spending $500 and earn 5% of every purchase back as points. Customers reach Gold status after spending $2000 and earn 10% of every purchase back as points. Gold members also receive priority customer support.

## Data privacy
Customer personal data is retained for 24 months after the last purchase and is then anonymised. Customers may request deletion of their data at any time.
