# Customer Service Playbook

## Response times
Emails must be answered within 24 hours. Live chat must be answered within 2 minutes. Phone calls must be answered within 30 seconds during opening hours.

## Escalation
Complaints that are still unresolved after 48 hours must be escalated to a supervisor. Customers affected by a serious product defect are escalated immediately.

## Compensation
A voucher of up to $25 may be given to customers whose delivery was delayed by more than 3 days. Larger compensation needs manager approval.

## Win-back campaigns
Customers who have been inactive for 60 days should receive a 10% voucher by email. If there is no purchase within 30 days after the voucher, the customer moves to the dormant list.

## Customer surveys
A satisfaction survey is sent 3 days after delivery. Scores below 3 out of 5 trigger a follow-up call within 24 hours.
